## Objective

Commission a professional designer to turn the current visual references into a coherent, ownable, accessible brand system for Alkaest and Alkaest Chat. The designer should preserve the project's distinct visual direction while making it usable across product interfaces and contributor materials.

The existing reference boards are creative direction only. Embedded claims, text, quotations, or depictions of future capabilities are not approved product messaging.

## Draft deliverables

1. A concise brand rationale and visual direction covering the Alkaest system mark and its relationship to Alkaest Chat.
2. Editable vector master assets for the approved marks, including full-color, single-color, light/dark, and small-size variants where appropriate.
3. A documented color and typography system with accessible contrast guidance and editable design tokens in an agreed format.
4. Symbol construction and clear-space/minimum-size guidance, plus practical do/don't examples.
5. Application examples for the website/contributor page and app UI, including responsive and small-screen use.
6. Source files and a delivery inventory, with third-party fonts/assets and their licenses clearly listed.
7. A written rights/license arrangement that permits Alkaest to use, modify, publish, and maintain the accepted deliverables, agreed before work begins.

## Acceptance criteria to freeze before assignment

- Every agreed deliverable is present in the agreed editable/source formats and opens in the named tools.
- Alkaest and Alkaest Chat are visually related but distinguishable; their hierarchy matches the guide.
- The system works against light and dark surfaces and at the agreed interface sizes; contrast and non-color affordances are documented.
- The deliverables include usage guidance and real application examples, not only a presentation board.
- Font, stock, and other third-party asset dependencies are disclosed with valid license terms; no unlicensed music, imagery, or typeface is included.
- All required file revisions, presentation sessions, revision rounds, and final delivery date are explicitly listed in the published WorkItem.
- Acceptance is recorded by a named, non-conflicted human reviewer against these criteria. Aesthetic judgment may be part of review, but an AI score or the founder's payment action alone is not the network's validation record.

Before publication, replace this line with explicit milestone-level acceptance checks if payment is staged. Each payable milestone must be separately described, budgeted, and funded before work on that milestone begins.

## Intended lifecycle exercise

When the protocol exists, use this WorkItem to test:

1. Publish terms and collect contributor questions before assignment.
2. Record founder funding intent, then confirm actual funding reservation only through the approved treasury and escrow mechanism.
3. Assign one designer under the exclusive-assignment MVP rule.
4. Submit versioned artifacts and provenance evidence; keep private contract and payment details private.
5. Record independent human review against the frozen criteria, including a disputed path.
6. Authorize only the predeclared amount and recipient; mark settled only after independent settlement verification.
7. Reconcile unspent or refunded funds and export the complete signed audit trail.

The first rehearsal must use simulated balances or Bitcoin `regtest`. No real deposit or payment should be attempted until the applicable RFC-0003, RFC-0008, RFC-0013, and RFC-0014 launch gates and independent security review are complete.
