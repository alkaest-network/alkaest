# Code of Conduct

Alkaest welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and viewpoints. Participation in project spaces, issues, pull requests, and events is conditional on treating others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, and changes rather than people.
- Make space for different experiences and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or private communications without permission.
- Follow the repository's security-reporting guidance when sharing a vulnerability.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or communications without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussion or repeatedly ignoring reasonable moderation requests.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in Alkaest project repositories and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. They may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. To raise a concern, contact a maintainer through the contact method on the [Alkaest Network GitHub organization](https://github.com/alkaest-network). Do not include sensitive details in a public report. If no private contact method is available, open an issue asking for one without naming people or describing the incident.
