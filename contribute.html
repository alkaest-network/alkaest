The protocol · One core, many kinds of work

Coordination, with its parts in the open.

Alkaest gives independent participants a shared way to describe work, record what happened, and carry verifiable history between contexts.

Evidence before scorePortable · Domain-aware · Verifiable

People own their identity

Reputation follows a cryptographic identity, not one device or platform account.

Nodes keep the network plural

Participants can run software and retain control over their own place in the network.

Modules describe the domain

Open-source work is the first workflow. The core stays useful beyond one market.

Evidence gives history context

Outcomes can be checked and interpreted over time, instead of reduced to a context-free rating.
